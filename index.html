RNA quality control in aging cells.

Research

How does nuclear RNA quality control break down with age?

I work in the Barral laboratory at ETH Zürich using budding yeast as a model for cellular aging. My research focuses on pre-mRNA retention, nuclear-pore remodeling, introns, and the consequences of allowing incompletely processed transcripts to reach the cytoplasm.

The broader question is simple: cells invest heavily in deciding which RNAs are ready to leave the nucleus. I want to understand how that decision changes as a cell ages or encounters stress, and how those changes become physiology.
